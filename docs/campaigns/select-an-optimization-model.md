## Optimization Engine Options

You can use this information to decide which Optimization model, or Optimizer, to use for your campaign: Clicks, Conversions, or Revenue. 
 
1. Click Optimizer

Optimized Metric: CTR (Clickthrough Rate)

Can I control my billed CPC? Yes. The CPC bid you enter will not vary.  

Is there a learning period? No. Select, launch, and go!  

Conversions Optimizer

Optimized Metric: CPO (Cost per Order)  

Can I control my billed CPC? Partially. The CPC bid you enter will slightly vary. If you don’t want to bid higher than a specific amount, you can enter a maximum bid (different than the CPC bid you enter) that will act as a hard cap.  

Is there a learning period? Yes, it can take 7-10 days on average before the optimizer reaches maximum efficiency.  

:::note
If you decide to change your optimizer to the Revenue Optimizer, your line item will re-enter the learning phase for 7-10 days. If you return to the Conversion Optimizer at a later date, the line item will also need to re-enter the learning phase.
:::

Revenue Optimizer

Optimized Metric: ROAS (Return On Ad Spend)

Can I control my billed CPC? Partially. The CPC bid you enter will slightly vary. If you don’t want to bid higher than a specific amount, you can enter a maximum bid (different than the CPC bid you enter) that will act as a hard cap.  

Is there a learning period? Yes, it can take 7-10 days on average before the optimizer reaches maximum efficiency. 
:::note
If you decide to change your optimizer to the Conversion Optimizer, your line item will re-enter the learning phase for 7-10 days. If you return to the Revenue Optimizer at a later date, the line item will also need to re-enter the learning phase.
:::
