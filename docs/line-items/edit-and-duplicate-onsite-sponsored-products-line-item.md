## Edit and Duplicate Onsite Sponsored Products Line Item

In this guide, you will learn how to edit and duplicate line items for Onsite Sponsored Products. If you have already created your line item and want to make edits or duplicate it, follow the steps.

## Edit a Sponsored Product Line Item

In this section, you will go through the process of how you can edit a line item for Sponsored Products. Once you've created your line item, you can still edit most of its settings.

In the Campaigns tab, click the campaign whose line items you wish to edit. It will take you to the Line Item dashboard, where you can find the following subtabs:

Line Item: You can edit the line item level settings, such as Name, Flight Dates, Budget, Pacing, Bid, or Optimization Strategy, by clicking on the edit icon (pencil) next to each field.

Campaign Products - You can manage all products within a campaign through a dedicated tab that consolidates global SKUs across retailer-specific line items. This view allows you to review and adjust each product's association with individual line items and view aggregated performance metrics.

Campaign Settings – You can edit all the campaign-level settings like Name, Schedule, Budget, Attribution, etc.

To edit a line item, follow the steps below:

Click on the Campaigns tab within the platform.

Find the campaign containing the line item you wish to edit and click on the Campaign Name.

You'll land on your campaign’s Line Items page, where you can see all its line items. You can click a line item’s name to open the line item, and you can click the Edit icon in each column to edit fields like the Name, Budget, Line Item Start and End date, etc.

If you wish to view the settings of a specific line item and make edits, click on the desired line item. On the left side of the dashboard, you will see the following subpages where you can review the setup and edit them:

Products: View all the products that are added to the Line Item. You can select the products to switch them On/OFF, and also Edit Bid or Remove Bid.

Placements: View all the placements where the ads in the line item are running.

Keywords: View all the active keywords. You can also see the Automatic Keywords - these are the most relevant keywords for the selected products in your line item.

Edit: Under the Edit page, you can edit all the settings that you had initially applied while creating the line item.

## Duplicate a Sponsored Product Line Item

This guide walks you through the steps to duplicate an Onsite Sponsored Products line item. After creating a line item, you can duplicate it and make any necessary adjustments. 
To duplicate a Line Item, follow these steps:

From the Campaigns dashboard, select the campaign that contains the line item you want to duplicate. This will open the Line Item dashboard. 

In the Actions column, click the "…" icon on the far right of the line item. 

Click Duplicate. A new line item will be created with the same settings as the original. 

Update the name and flight dates as needed, making sure they remain within the campaign’s date range. 

The duplicated line item will appear in draft status, ready for your edits. 

Follow the workflow steps through to the Review page, then click Launch to go live.
Note:  
The Retailer field cannot be changed—even in duplicated line items. If you need to change the retailer, you must create a new line item from scratch.

## Step 1: Settings

Editing a line item name
Click the edit icon in the Line Item column in the table to be able to edit the line item name. Alternatively, you can click the line item's name and then click Edit in the left panel to land on the Settings page of the line item editing process, where you can edit your line item's name.

Editing a line item retailer
In Step 1 of your line item editing workflow, you can see the previously selected retailer. The input field under the retailer box is greyed (disabled). This is because you cannot edit the retailer selected for your line item after launching it.
Note: If you need to select another retailer, we recommend that you set up a new line item with the new retailer and product(s). 
European Markets Only: Edit Targeting Based on Tracking Preference   
If your line item is tied to a retailer who has enabled this feature, you will find an additional section for Targeting Based on Tracking Preference settings. These controls allow you to choose whether you want your line item to serve both opted-in and opted-out shoppers. By default, and unless these settings are edited, your line item will serve to opt-in shoppers only. If you see no settings available to edit and are a non-Read Only user, you can assume that you are only targeting opted-in shoppers. You may adjust your preferences here at any point. 
 
Important: As mentioned above, no sales attribution is collected for opted-out users, as attribution relies on the user ID. Therefore, ROAS and conversion-based KPIs will likely be lower on line items targeting opted-in and opted-out shoppers. However, targeting both opted-in and opted-out shoppers increases total impressions and clicks. If you change your settings, be aware that your performance is likely to change.

Editing Bid Strategy
To edit the Bid Strategy, click the edit icon against the Bid Strategy Column in the table. You can choose from Adaptive or Standard. After you edit it, you'll need to update your schedule, budget, and CPC bid settings in the next steps.

Editing Schedule
Click on the Edit icon next to the line item dates column in the table, or in Step 1 of the line item editing workflow, you can see the setup dates under the Schedule box.  
 
To edit the dates, select new dates and Save. You can choose today’s date or any date in the future for the start date. For the end date, select either None or a specific date. Click on Save before exiting.

Editing Schedule in Bulk
To edit the flight dates for multiple line items at once, from the line Items dashboard, you can select the checkbox next to each line item’s ON/OFF toggle.  
You can select all line items by clicking the checkbox in the header row (above the Total row). If any checkbox is selected, an indicator showing how many line items are currently selected will appear above the header row, along with an Edit option.  
Once all the line items for which you wish to adjust flights dates for are selected, click Edit. You can toggle the line items ON or OFF, or click Edit Start/End date.  
Note: A change in your line item dates can cause a change in its status as well.

## Step 2: Keyword Targeting Strategy

To edit the keywords, click the line item's name and select Keywords from the left panel.

Click the Keywords button in the right-hand corner

Enter your desired keywords and click Submit.
Note:  The keywords you submit will be reviewed for approval
To be approved, each submitted keyword must be relevant to all the products in this line item. If you submit irrelevant keywords, they’ll be rejected.

Line Item Editing Workflow
Click Edit in the left panel to land on step 1 of the line item editing workflow. Click Next on Step 1 to reach the keyword targeting strategy step, where you will see the types of keywords you can choose for your line item to target:

Generic, Branded, and Conquesting

Generic and Branded

Conquesting

These options are greyed out or disabled, as you cannot edit your keyword targeting strategy after the line item has been launched.

However, in the Additional Keywords section, you can add keywords manually to the Negative or Submitted Keywords fields.
From here, you can enter the keyword(s) you want to exclude your ad from appearing in the Negative Keywords section. Simply add or remove inputs to edit the selection.
 
When you're done, be sure to navigate through all the line item steps, up to the Review step, and click on Launch. Otherwise, your negative keywords will not be taken into account. 
 
Negative Keywords may be entered as either: 

Broad Match
Negate search terms that include the keyword or continuous keyword string, including all stemmed variations of the keyword(s).
Example: If men’s shoes are a negative keyword, your ad won’t show for blue men’s shoes, but it may show for shoes and men’s basketball shoes (as basketball breaks up the continuous phrase). Your ads may continue to serve for search terms that include additional keywords within the keyword string - but not before or after a continuous negative keyword string - or that don’t include all keywords in a negative keyword string.

Exact Match
Negate search terms that are the exact keyword or keyword string, including all the stemmed variations of the keyword(s). Your ads may continue to serve search terms that include any additional keywords or that don’t include all keywords in a negative keyword string. 
Note: They will be applied to your line item within 15-30 minutes after you save. 
 
Tips: 

Enter one keyword or keyword string per row. 

Keywords are not case-sensitive. 

Keywords will be applied to all products targeted in the line item; therefore, to negate keywords for only certain products, create a separate line item for these products. 

Duplicate entries will be automatically de-duplicated. 

The same keyword entered in both match-type textboxes will be considered as two keywords. For example, “remote control” in the Broad Match textbox and “remote control” in the Exact Match textbox are counted as two keywords. 

Negative keywords take priority. Meaning if a keyword is in both the Negative Keyword section and the Submitted Keyword section, it will be treated as a Negated Keyword. 

Up to 1,000 Broad Match and up to 1,000 Exact Match negative keywords can be entered per line item. 

Special characters are permitted but may automatically be parsed.

## Step 3: Products

Viewing and Removing Products
You can see and manage all products in one place, even if they appear in multiple line items. For each product, you can check which retailers it's linked to, view its performance, and see the bid amount being used (or the range if it's in more than one line item).
If you want to stop or start a product to serve on all the product-related line items, you can select one or multiple products and toggle the switch On/Off.

To view or edit the line items for any specific product:  

Under the Campaign Products page, click on the Line Item number you see for the respective product under the Line Items column. 

You can turn the product ON or OFF across all retailers at once or manage its ON/OFF status for each retailer separately. If needed, you can update bid amounts for the product across all retailers in one go.  

To add any new products to the existing line items:  

Click on the +Add Multiple Products button on the Campaign Products page. 

You will be taken to the Products page, where you can add multiple products at once, either from the Account Catalog or by finding matches by Product IDs.   

If you search by keyword (Search Account Catalog), all products with a matching word in their name will be returned.   

With the product ID (Search by Product ID) option, you first select the type of product ID you're searching by and then enter the IDs (one per line). The search will return all exact matches, and let you know if some don’t exist.   

Select the desired product(s) and click on Next.  

You will now see the Line Items that exist in your campaign, along with the Retailers associated with each Line Item.  

Click the number in the Products column to review the selected products and their availability. 

If needed, you can update bid amounts for the product across all retailers in one go. 

The system will automatically place each SKU into the right line items based on which retailers carry that product.  

When you have verified all the changes made, click on Next, and the new products will be added.  
Important: If you’d like to add products that are not available on any of the retailers for which line items already exist, you will need to add via the regular new Line Item workflow, which you can launch on the Line Items tab once within a campaign. Please see Create an Onsite Sponsored Products Line Item for step-by-step instructions.  

Pause Product Delivery
You can toggle delivery for all selected products within a campaign or line item at any time. Pausing a product will stop the campaign and line item from delivering any ads for that product. Un-pausing will resume delivery for that product if it is in stock. You can toggle products On/Off at both the campaign and line item level.

At the Campaign Level:

On the campaign landing page, click on Campaign Products in the left-side navigation menu.

You will land on a page that shows you all the products that are being targeted within the campaign. Click the On/Off toggle next to any product to turn delivery for that product on or off.

At the Line Item Level:

From the campaign landing page, select the line item you want to adjust the delivery for.

Click on Details in the View column.

On the resulting products page, click the On/Off toggle next to each product to adjust the delivery for that product.
Important: 

After a product is paused, it will be removed from the default view of the product dashboard. To view the paused product in the data table, adjust the filter of the table to include “On and Off Products”.

Also, you cannot adjust the bid of a paused product. To adjust the bid of a paused product, you must first unpause it and then adjust the bid.

## Step 4: Bid

Edit a Bid:
In the Line Items dashboard, you can edit each line item’s Bid CPC directly in the Bid (CPC) column by clicking the Edit icon displayed to the right of the existing bid when you hover over it. To modify your bid, simply enter the amount in the input field in the Edit Bid Strategy menu.
Important: Bid Strategy can only be edited using the in-line editing option available on the Line Items dashboard, which displays all line items within a campaign. It is not possible to update it from the Edit Line Item setup workflow that opens when you choose to edit an individual line item.

Editing in Bulk
To edit bids for multiple line items at once:

Select the checkbox next to each line item’s ON/OFF toggle from the Line Items dashboard. You can select all line items by clicking the checkbox in the header row (above the Total row). If any checkbox is selected, an indicator showing how many line items are currently selected will appear above the header row, along with an Edit option.

Once all the line items whose bids you wish to edit are selected, click Edit. In addition to being able to turn the line items ON or OFF or edit their flight dates, you can also edit bids.

The bid editing functionality at the line item level gives users two options by which to adjust their bids:

By a set amount

By increasing or decreasing by % factor (Example: if my bid is $1 and I increase by +20%, my new bid will be $1.20)
If a line item has a Max Bid set and you wish to increase your line item or product bid overrides to a set amount or by a percentage factor that would result in a bid that exceeds the current Max CPC value, choosing the Increase Maximum Bid option allows the Max CPC bid to be updated simultaneously to match the new amount.
If this option is not selected, the increase will be capped such that the new bid will match the existing Max CPC value.
Lastly, if the proposed increase does not result in a new bid amount exceeding the current Max CPC value, selecting this option has no impact.

Suggested Bids
Suggested Bids is an intelligent tool that provides CPC Bid recommendations for Onsite Sponsored Products line items, based on the winning bids for products that are similar to your line item’s SKUs. A suggested bid range is displayed in the Bid section (step 3) of the line item setup workflow.

Once the line item goes live, the suggested range will also be displayed in the Edit Bid Strategy menu for each line item, where you can modify the CPC bid over the course of the flight.

The range is based on continuous real-time analysis of historical auction data within your line item’s SKUs categories – and specifically for existing line items, the auctions your line item specifically competed in.

Suggested Bids helps to simplify the optimization process by arming you with instant insight into competitor bids, to benchmark your own CPC against. This can help you more efficiently assess the right level at which to bid without risking overpaying relative to other advertisers in your category and ensure that your campaign starts winning auctions and reaching relevant shoppers immediately.

If you decide impressions are worth more than the suggested bid range, you can always raise your CPC higher, and your SKUs will be more likely to win auctions and serve onsite. Conversely, you can set your bid below the range until the Minimum Bid is reached.
Tip: The suggested bid range can be especially helpful in determining a bid to use when you first launch your line item, given that you won’t yet have historical performance. Because the guidance is based on the most recent winning bid values that led to an ad being displayed onsite for similar products competing in your relevant categories, you can use the range to ensure your line item is competitive from the outset, while avoiding overpaying for impressions.  
Note: If you edited your products, the minimum bid may be different now and you might have to update your bid if it is lower than the new minimum.

## Step 5: Review

The last step of the line item editing workflow. Once you have edited all the desired line item settings, you can review them in the last step: Review. Once final, click Launch to launch your line item. 

Line Item Status
A line item's status is determined by its configuration and by the manually controlled Off/On toggle that can be found in the line item table on the campaign details page.  
 
To view the status of your line item, follow the steps below: 

Click Campaigns in the header of the Commerce Max Platform to access the Campaign section. 

Find the campaign in which you want to edit a line item and click on its campaign name. 

You'll land on your campaign page, where you can see all line items and their status. 
A line item status can change if: 

It has been completed and saved 

Its start or end date has reached

Its daily or monthly pace is reached

The Off/On toggle is manually switched 

It has been inactive for the past 30 days

## Editing Line Item Budget

Line item budgets help you control your advertising costs for specific products. In Commerce Max, you can decide to either set a total budget for your line item or leave your line item uncapped. You can edit a line item budget at any time, effective immediately.

To edit the budget:

On the Campaigns page, click the name of the campaign that contains the line item you want to edit.

On the Line Items page, click the name of the line item you want to edit.

Click the Edit icon in the Remaining Budget column, or you can edit the budget in Step 1 of your line item workflow.

If switching from uncapped to capped, select Capped and enter the amount to cap the budget at a set amount. Once your line item reaches the set budget amount, it will stop delivering ads. 
Note: If you choose a budget amount higher than your campaign budget or if the sum of line item budget amounts in your campaign exceeds your campaign budget, your line item will stop delivering once the campaign budget is reached.

If switching from capped to uncapped, select Uncapped if you'd like your line item to spend freely from your campaign's budget.  

If editing your capped line item budget, choose Increase By or Decrease By and enter the amount to add to or remove from your line item's remaining budget. You can mark the New Remaining Budget checkbox to make sure the budget corresponds to the amount you want your line item to be able to spend.

Click Save.
Note: When editing a capped line item budget, you're adding or decreasing the remaining budget of your line item and not setting a new line item budget.
Example: If your line item budget is $700 and your line item costs reach $350, you have a remaining budget of $350. You'd like your line item to be able to spend $1,000 in total, so you add $300  to your remaining budget of $350, bringing the total remaining budget to $650. The $350 your line item already spent + $650 of the remaining budget = $1,000.
 
You have two options available for editing your budget: 

Uncapped budget: 
Select Uncapped. Your line item will spend freely from the funds available at the campaign level. The available funds are determined by the campaign budget and can be capped or uncapped. 
Your line item will stop spending once the campaign budget is reached. At this point, your line item status will switch to "Budget Hit". 

Capped budget: 
Click Capped and select either Increase by or Decrease by from the dropdown to enter the amount you’d like to add or remove from your current remaining budget. 
You can see the new remaining budget at the bottom of the line Item Budget box. 
Your line item might not spend the entire amount. The amount is used as a cap, and your line item will stop spending when it is reached. 

Editing a daily pace 
Click on the edit icon next to the line item daily budget column, or in Step 1 of your line item workflow, you can edit the pacing of your budget delivery.   
 
A daily pace can be helpful to ensure your budget lasts for the duration of your line item's schedule. 
To edit a daily pace, you have two options. 

Strict amount: 
Click on the amount line and enter an amount. 
This amount will serve as a daily cap. Every day, your line item will stop serving ads and switch to a “Budget Hit” status when this amount is reached. The line item will automatically reactivate at the start of the next day (as long as there are funds available for it to spend). 

Automate: 
Enter a line item end date and a total budget or a monthly pace in order to be able to activate the Automated option. The Automated option will adjust your daily pace at the beginning of each day based on the line item end date and remaining budget. 
Example: If your line item is set to last for 30 days and the total remaining line item budget is $30,000, then the Automated option will set a pace of $1,000 delivery per day. 
This option is helpful if the line item does not reach its daily spend every day, but you want to make sure it has the opportunity to spend the full budget. 
Example: Your line item underspends for the first 10 days, and on the  
$30,000 total budget, you still have $25,000 to spend in just 20 days.  
The automated daily pace will calculate the line item daily pace  
should be $1,250 per day to spend the remaining budget. With this option, you don't have to adjust your daily pace every day based on yesterday's spend; the Automated option will do it for you. 
 
Editing a monthly pace 
Click the edit icon next to the line item monthly budget column, or in Step 1 of your line item workflow, you can edit the pacing of your budget delivery.   
 
To edit a monthly pace, click on the amount line and enter a new amount. Click on Continue or Save before exiting.  
 
This amount will serve as a monthly cap. During the month, your line item will stop serving ads and switch to a “Budget Hit” status when this amount is reached. The line item will automatically reactivate at the start of the next month (as long as there are funds available for it to spend). 
Note: If you choose a budget amount higher than your campaign budget or if the sum of line item budget amounts in your campaign exceeds your campaign budget, your line item will stop delivering once the campaign budget is reached.
